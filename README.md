# Conexa Remote 🍋🤝

> **Direct WebRTC P2P Remote Desktop.** Native sub-50ms latency, end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer streaming, 100% EU GDPR data sovereignty, and fair concurrent session licensing.

Official Website: **[conexaremote.com](https://conexaremote.com)**

---

### ⚡ Architecture & Highlights

- **Direct WebRTC P2P:** Direct peer-to-peer data channel streaming without central relay bottlenecks.
- **Sub-50ms Latency:** High-performance hardware video capture & encoding (Windows DXGI Desktop Duplication, macOS ScreenCaptureKit) for fluid 60 FPS remote management.
- **Enterprise Security:** TLS 1.3 signaling + DTLS/SRTP end-to-end encryption. No intermediate servers record or relay video streams.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Signaling & STUN/TURN infrastructure 100% hosted in the European Union (Romania). No third-party trackers.
- **Fair Pricing Model:** Permanent free tier (1 concurrent connection, zero timeouts). Professional tier at 29 €/mo for 2 concurrent active sessions (+5 €/mo per extra concurrent slot) with unlimited technician accounts and unlimited endpoint deployments.
